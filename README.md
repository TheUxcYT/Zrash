## ✨ What is ZRash?

**ZRash** is a modern practice core for Minecraft servers. It gives players a fast, fair, and customizable environment to train PvP mechanics — combo, aim, CPS, strafing, bridging, and more — while giving server owners a clean, configurable, and lag-free plugin.

Whether you're running a public practice server, a private training arena, or a competitive scrim hub, ZRash stays out of your way and just works.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **⚡ Optimized core** — async-friendly, minimal tick impact, no lag spikes.
- **🗡️ Combat training** — 1v1 duels, bot fights, and combat dummies.
- **🎯 Aim & CPS trainer** — target tracking, reaction time, and CPS stats.
- **🌉 Bridging & movement** — godbridge, breezily, and scaffold practice maps.
- **🏆 Ranked & unranked queues** — ELO-based matchmaking with fair pairings.
- **🧩 Kit system** — NoDebuff, Sumo, Gapple, Boxing, BuildUHC, Combo, and more.
- **📊 Player stats** — wins, losses, K/D, ELO, streaks, and per-kit breakdowns.
- **🎨 Fully configurable** — YAML-driven kits, arenas, messages, and scoreboard.
- **🔌 Addon API** — extend kits, queues, and stats via a simple event API.
- **🛡️ Anti-cheat friendly** — clean hooks, no packet abuse, no conflicts.
- **🌍 Multi-version** — supports 1.8 through 1.21 via NMS abstraction.
This core is under dev!
